Do Off-Amazon Sellers Want Faster Shipping Speeds?

Read the case study

Business question

Should Amazon invest in same-day or sub-same-day shipping for off-Amazon sellers for 2026–2028 planning?

My role

  • End-to-end owner: framing, design, fieldwork, synthesis, exec readouts
  • Resolved director/principal-level disagreement blocking roadmap decisions
  • Aligned Product, Ops, AFN, SCOT, and NATS stakeholders

What I did

  • Analyzed existing signals (7,000+ feedback echoes) and performance (pilot + broad rollout)
  • Ran 11× 90-minute interviews with SMB DTC merchants and logistics leaders
  • Focused on willingness to pay, operational constraints, and speed/flexibility tradeoffs

What changed

Strategy shifted from “faster is always better” to a configurable fulfillment framework balancing speed, cost, and operational control (crawl/walk/run roadmap framing).

Key insights (scannable)

  • Limited demand for faster-than-3-day shipping; 2–3 day options met expectations.
  • Cost constraints dominated; ROI for faster speeds was unproven for conversion/retention.
  • Merchants prioritized flexibility (branding, bundling, edits, routing) over raw speed.
  • Competitive pressure on speed was decreasing (slower windows normalized by some marketplaces).
